GST Calculation (12%):

The cart now correctly calculates:

  • Total (Inclusive) = Sum of all (Price × Quantity)
  • GST (12%) = (Total × 12) / 112
  • Subtotal (Exclusive) = Total - GST

Example:

  • Product 1: ₹1,000 × 1 = ₹1,000
  • Product 2: ₹155 × 1 = ₹155
  • Total (Incl. GST): ₹1,155.00
  • GST (12%): ₹123.75
  • Subtotal (Excl. GST): ₹1,031.25
